Operating Guidelines:
For spray jobs carrying restricted chemicals (such as Glyphosate), maximum speed must not exceed 12 km/h.

Maintenance: Nozzle inspection required every 50 operating hours.

Optimal tilling speed is 8 km/h. Fuel consumption increases significantly above 2200 RPM.

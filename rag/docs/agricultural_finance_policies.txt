[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-101]
Policy: Agricultural Credit Standing & Account Eligibility
Category: Underwriting & Eligibility
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Baseline Account Standing: Applicants must maintain an active, unencumbered customer account with zero active credit holds (credit_hold = 0).
2. Past-Due Balance Threshold: Any outstanding overdue balance exceeding $500 triggers an automated underwriting block until cleared.
3. Remediation Protocol: Customers under credit hold must provide proof of full settlement or enter an authorized 60-day debt rehabilitation agreement prior to new facility underwriting.
4. Operational History: A minimum of 1 full operating season (or verified historical crop harvest receipts) is required for unsecured credit lines.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-102]
Policy: Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) & Cashflow Underwriting Standards
Category: Financial Capacity & Risk Assessment
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Minimum DSCR Requirements:
   - Standard Commercial Agricultural Loans: Minimum DSCR of 1.25x (Net Farm Operating Income / Annual Debt Service).
   - Subsidized & Development Facilities: Minimum DSCR of 1.15x.
2. DSCR Calculation: Calculated as (Gross Farm Revenue - Operating Expenses + Depreciation) / (Annual Principal + Interest Payments).
3. Risk Classification:
   - DSCR >= 1.40: Low Risk (eligible for streamlined automated approval).
   - DSCR 1.25 - 1.39: Medium Risk (standard loan structures, standard collateral covenants).
   - DSCR < 1.25: High Risk (requires senior credit committee / HITL review, supplemental collateral, or secondary co-signer).
4. Maximum Debt Ceiling: Aggregate farm debt service must not exceed 45% of gross annual farm revenue.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-103]
Policy: Agricultural Loan-to-Value (LTV) & Asset Collateral Standards
Category: Collateral & Security
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Maximum LTV Limits by Asset Class:
   - Real Estate & Titled Farm Land: Maximum 80% LTV.
   - New Machinery & Heavy Utility Tractors: Maximum 75% LTV.
   - Used Machinery (> 3 years operating age): Maximum 65% LTV.
   - Precision Sprayers, Chemical Applicators & Implements: Maximum 70% LTV with mandatory annual maintenance certification.
2. Harvest & Crop Liens: First-priority crop liens against registered field harvest yield agreements may be pledged for working capital facilities up to 50% of projected farm-gate revenue.
3. Subordination: Secondary liens require explicit written authorization from senior lienholders.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-104]
Policy: Mandatory KYC & Underwriting Verification Document Checklist
Category: Document Validation & Compliance
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Required Verification Documents:
   - Government-issued Photo Identification (Passport, National ID, or Driver's License) for all primary farm owners.
   - 2 to 3 years of filed Farm Tax Returns (IRS Schedule F or regional agricultural tax equivalent).
   - Past 6 months of consecutive business operating bank account statements.
   - Proof of Land Ownership (registered title/deed) or Certified Multi-Year Agricultural Lease Agreement for all designated operational parcels.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-301]
Policy: Agricultural Machinery Lease vs. Purchase vs. Custom-Hire Decision Matrix
Category: Equipment Advisory & Strategy
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Operating Lease Recommendation:
   - Optimal when annual machinery utilization is under 400 operating engine hours per season.
   - Recommended when farmer prioritizes preserving working capital for variable inputs (seed, chemical, fertilizer).
   - Full factory warranty, scheduled maintenance, and depreciation risk retained by lessor. 100% tax-deductible as operating expense.
2. Capital Lease / Chattel Mortgage Loan Recommendation:
   - Optimal when equipment utilization exceeds 600 operating engine hours per year across multiple fields.
   - Recommended for core tractors (TRC-2001) where long-term asset retention (> 7 years) and balance sheet equity building are desired.
3. Custom-Hire / Co-op Equipment Share Recommendation:
   - Optimal for small parcels (< 50 acres) or specialized single-use implements (e.g. grain harvesters) where capital carrying cost per acre exceeds rental market rates.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-302]
Policy: Machinery Maintenance Reserve & Fleet Lifecycle Policy
Category: Operational Advisory
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Mandatory Operating Reserves:
   - Heavy Utility Tractors: $12.00 - $16.00 per operating hour maintenance reserve.
   - High-Clearance Chemical Sprayers: $15.00 - $22.00 per operating hour maintenance reserve (inclusive of nozzle pressure recalibration every 50 hours).
2. Economic Replacement Lifecycle: Optimal machinery trade-in / replacement occurs at 5,000 engine operating hours or 5 crop seasons to maximize residual trade-in equity and avoid exponential maintenance depreciation.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-401]
Policy: Credit Risk Tiering & Interest Rate Pricing Structure
Category: Pricing & Terms
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Tier 1 (Prime / Low Risk):
   - Criteria: DSCR >= 1.40, LTV <= 65%, clean credit history (0 delinquencies).
   - Pricing: 4.5% - 5.5% APR. Standard term 24-48 months.
   - Approval: Automated underwriting approval.
2. Tier 2 (Standard / Medium Risk):
   - Criteria: DSCR 1.25 - 1.39, LTV 65% - 75%.
   - Pricing: 5.5% - 7.0% APR. Standard term 36-60 months.
   - Approval: Automated approval for amounts < $50,000.
3. Tier 3 (Subprime / High Risk):
   - Criteria: DSCR < 1.25, LTV > 75%, or loan amount >= $50,000.
   - Pricing: 7.0% - 9.0% APR.
   - Approval: Requires mandatory Senior Credit Committee / Human Admin review.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-402]
Policy: Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Senior Credit Committee Escalation Thresholds
Category: Governance & Risk Controls
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Mandatory HITL Review Triggers:
   - Any financing application with requested/assessed principal amount >= $50,000.00.
   - Any application classified as High Risk (DSCR < 1.25x or high debt burden).
   - Any case where an agronomy or equipment domain specialist flagged specialized engineering/operational risk.
2. Senior Admin Authority:
   - Approve: Releases application to external lender submission.
   - Reject: Formulates structured constructive rejection notice with remediation criteria.
   - Request More Info: Returns application to document collection for supplemental verification or secondary collateral.

Policy: Harvest-Synchronized Seasonal Amortization & Repayment Covenants
Category: Repayment Terms & Cashflow Management
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Cashflow Alignment: Repayment schedules for crop production and agricultural equipment must mirror seasonal liquidity.
2. Payment Frequency Options:

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-502]
Policy: Drought, Climate & Yield Loss Hardship Forbearance Policy
Category: Hardship & Risk Management
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Hardship Relief: In the event of a federally or regionally declared agricultural natural disaster (drought, flood, hail, pest outbreak), affected farmers in good standing qualify for up to 6 months principal and interest forbearance.
2. Capitalization Waiver: Interest accrued during the approved emergency forbearance window is not capitalized into loan principal.
3. Term Extension: Loan maturity horizon extended by the exact duration of the forbearance period.

[DOC_ID: SOP-FIN-503]
Policy: Fallback Structuring & Prepayment Penalty Waiver Policy
Category: Alternative Structuring
Rules & Guidelines:
1. Provider Rejection Fallbacks: If an external lending provider declines terms, the system must generate structured alternatives:
   - Downsized Principal Facility (reduced by 25-35% to achieve DSCR compliance).
   - Co-operative Machinery Lease or Custom-Hire alternative.
   - Phased disbursement structured around verified seasonal input milestones.

CROP DISEASE IDENTIFICATION AND TREATMENT PROTOCOLS
Greenfield Agricultural Agency — Agronomy Division

DOC_ID: CROP-PROTO-001
Policy: Foliar Fungal Diseases of Corn (Leaf Blight Complex)
Category: Disease Diagnosis & Treatment

Rules & Guidelines:
1. Identification: Gray-green water-soaked lesions developing into long elliptical
   brown streaks with yellow halos on leaves indicate Corn Leaf Blight (fungal).
   Yellow striping with scattered brown necrotic spots plus edge wilting indicates
   early-stage fungal leaf spot complex.
2. First-line treatment: Apply Neem Oil Extract as a low-hazard foliar spray at the
   first appearance of lesions. Neem Oil Extract is approved for all zones without
   senior sign-off.
3. Second-line treatment: For established infections covering more than 30 percent
   of leaf area, escalate to Chlorpyrifos-based fungicide application. Chlorpyrifos
   is a controlled-hazard chemical and REQUIRES senior agronomist sign-off before
   any dispatch.
4. Application method: Use a high-clearance sprayer. Tractors alone must never be
   dispatched for fungicide spraying jobs.
5. Re-treatment window: Re-inspect the treated field after 7 days. If lesions have
   stopped spreading, classify as improved. If leaf tissue is green again with no
   active lesions, classify as recovered. If lesions continue spreading, classify
   as worsened and restart diagnosis.

DOC_ID: CROP-PROTO-002
Policy: Contact Burndown and Weed Pressure in Row Crops
Category: Chemical Application

Rules & Guidelines:
1. Identification: Uniform yellowing and wilting across weed competition zones with
   stunted crop rows indicates weed pressure rather than fungal disease.
2. Treatment: Systemic burndown with Glyphosate applied by sprayer. Glyphosate is a
   RESTRICTED-hazard chemical and REQUIRES senior agronomist sign-off before dispatch.
3. Buffer rule: Never apply Glyphosate within 15 meters of irrigation canals.

DOC_ID: CROP-PROTO-003
Policy: Nutrient Deficiency Correction
Category: Soil & Foliar Nutrition

Rules & Guidelines:
1. Identification: Uniform pale-yellow striping along the whole leaf WITHOUT brown
   necrotic spots indicates nitrogen deficiency, not disease.
2. Treatment: Apply Foliar Fertilizer by sprayer. Foliar Fertilizer is low-hazard
   and requires no sign-off.
3. Follow-up: Re-inspect after 10 days; recovered color classifies the case as
   recovered.
